# Archival and Media Source Directory

This file is the acquisition map for the Marburgum art-reference archive. Sources are ranked by how directly they can answer questions about 1990s Maribor.

## Tier A — primary Maribor / Slovenian institutional sources

### Kamra — regional cultural heritage portal
https://www.kamra.si/

Kamra aggregates digitised local-history material from Slovenian libraries, archives and museums and is currently the most immediately productive online source for item-level Maribor photographs. Search both individual multimedia items and digital collections.

High-value starting collections/items:

- **Stari most 1991–2001** — https://www.kamra.si/digitalne-zbirke/stari-most-1991-2001/
- **Stari most: Maribor, dravski mostovi (1990–1991)** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/stari-most-maribor-dravski-mostovi-razglednica/
- **Bogo Čerin: Tanki v Mariboru (1991)** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/bogo-cerin-tank-v-mariboru/
- **Pekre: tu se je začelo** — https://www.kamra.si/en/digitalne-zbirke/pekre-tu-se-je-zacelo-2/
- **Podhod pod Mlinsko ulico, Maribor, 30 June 1991** — https://www.kamra.si/en/multimedia/podhod-pod-mlinsko-ulico-maribor-30-junij-1992/
- **Preizkušanje sanitetnega vozila (21 March 1990)** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/preizkusanje-sanitetnega-vozila/
- **Maribor, Glavni trg s kužnim znamenjem (1990s)** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/maribor-glavni-trg-s-kuznim-znamenjem/
- **TVT Nova digital collection / Studenci** — https://www.kamra.si/hu/digitalis-gyujtemenyek/tvt-nova-3/
- **DMV 813/814 (1990s)** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/dmv-813-814/
- **COBISS wall calendar for 1997** — https://www.kamra.si/mm-elementi/cobiss/
- **Prebujanje plesne dejavnosti v Mariboru** — item-level 1995–1997 dance/cultural material including Narodni dom reporting and portraits from the Zveza kulturnih društev archive.

Kamra item pages often expose location, date, collection relation, creator and copyright status, making them unusually suitable for catalogue ingestion. The TVT Nova material is particularly useful for Studenci industrial reconstruction, but several images are dated only to “the 1990s or first decade of the 2000s”; keep that uncertainty in downstream asset notes rather than treating the whole collection as securely 1990s.

### Univerzitetna knjižnica Maribor (UKM) — Digital Repository / local-history collections
https://dr.ukm.um.si/

UKM's digital repository contains a large body of image material and local-history collections. Relevant holdings include the Zbirka drobnih tiskov, photographic estates, local-history material and images associated with Maribor institutions and public life.

Search strategy:

- year filters **1990–1999**;
- place **Maribor**;
- material type **Slikovna gradiva / Fotografije**;
- district, street and landmark names;
- photographers' names discovered through Kamra;
- institutions and events rather than only generic "Maribor".

For non-digitised or incomplete holdings, preserve the UKM signature / COBISS identifier in the catalogue and treat it as an acquisition lead.

A useful **public-art/world-state chronology** is also exposed through UKM's Kamra collections: the Fran Miklošič bronze portrait head at Miklošičeva/Gospejna is dated to **3 October 1991**, while Drago Tršar's bust inside UKM was unveiled on **6 February 1997**. These exact dates are valuable for date-aware street/interior variants even when separate period photographs are still needed for appearance details.

### Pokrajinski arhiv Maribor (PAM)
https://www.pokarh-mb.si/
https://www.pokarh-mb.si/za-uporabnike/citalnica

PAM is essential for photographs, postcards, urban-planning documentation, building plans and municipal records. Its Virtual Archival Reading Room can be used to discover and request material.

Especially relevant fonds/collection families already encountered in Maribor research:

- **SI_PAM/1693 — Zbirka fotografij in razglednic**;
- **SI_PAM/1889 — Zavod za urbanizem Maribor**;
- municipal building / regulation and urban-planning records;
- building-plan collections;
- digitised photo-album and postcard material;
- industrial-company photographic fonds and documentation, especially **Metalna Maribor, TAM, Hidromontaža Maribor and Jeklotehna Maribor**.

PAM director Nina Gostenčnik has stated that, beyond more than 20,000 catalogued photographs and postcards, thousands of additional photographs survive in company fonds including Metalna, TAM, Hidromontaža and Jeklotehna. Treat those fonds as a high-priority acquisition path for factory interiors, yards, workers, machinery, signage and the physical effects of 1990s deindustrialisation; exact archival units still need to be identified and dated in VAČ or the reading room.

PAM should be queried when a model needs authoritative facade/plan evidence or when a 1990s photograph is not online.


### Europeana — Pokrajinski arhiv Maribor aggregation
https://www.europeana.eu/lv/collections/organisation/2335-regional-archives-maribor

Europeana exposes hundreds of PAM records in a browsable item-level layer and can reveal records that are difficult to discover by ordinary web search. Particularly useful current leads include **Poslovna stavba Jeklotehne v novoletni okrasitvi, 1990** and Jeklotehna shopfront records for Železnina (Jurčičeva 5), Barve-laki (Glavni trg 20) and Usnje (Partizanska 21). The shop records must not be assumed to be from 1990 merely because they appear beside the dated 1990 item; verify their individual dates in PAM before promoting them to direct 1990s evidence.

Use Europeana as a discovery/index layer and preserve the underlying PAM provenance and identifier in the catalogue.


### Ministrstvo za kulturo RS — INDOK / eHeritage heritage photography
https://www.eheritage.si/

The Ministry of Culture's INDOK photographic holdings are a newly identified **high-value exact-period architectural source**. Unlike many retrospective heritage pages, the digitised records can expose photographer, year, heritage identifier and a direct image of the registered building or monument.

Confirmed Maribor examples from **1991** include:

- **Cerkev sv. Alojzija, Glavni trg 8** — Damjan Gale, 1991; facade photograph also exposed through Europeana;
- **Kužno znamenje, Glavni trg** — Damjan Gale, 1991; at least two separately identified photographic views, SI_MK_MKS-003-66314 and SI_MK_MKS-003-66315.

For historically persistent landmarks, these are often stronger colour/finish references than postcards because they were made as systematic heritage documentation. Search the official heritage register/eHeritage by **Maribor + EŠD + year**, catalogue each useful view separately, and retain the INDOK local/global identifier. Do not infer redistribution rights from public visibility; Europeana currently marks the St Aloysius image as in copyright.



### Muzej narodne osvoboditve Maribor (MNOM)
https://mnom.si/
https://mnom.si/o-muzeju/

MNOM covers 20th- and 21st-century Maribor and explicitly maintains a **photograph library with photographs, negatives, video and audio material**. This makes it a priority institution not only for still imagery but for ambient sound, speech, events and moving-image reference.

Acquisition targets:

- everyday city scenes;
- industry and labour;
- subcultures and social life;
- independence-era documentation;
- shops, interiors, consumer objects and clothing;
- audio/video material that reveals movement, traffic, soundscape and lighting better than still images.

When useful material is not online, record a catalogue lead and contact the institution rather than inventing a substitute.

### Večer archive / Večer photojournalism

Večer is indispensable because a large share of 1990s Maribor's ordinary urban life was documented by local press photographers. Some Večer material appears through Kamra, UKM, publications and other institutional collaborations.

Known high-value leads include:

- Danilo Škofič's 30 June 1991 Mlinska/bus-station image via Kamra;
- Pekre/independence collections assembled from the **Arhiv Večera** and UKM;
- Dragan Grašič's **Metalna workers' strike, Maribor, December 1990**, cited as held by the Večer archive;
- the historical **Večer '98** CD-ROM, reported in 1999 as containing that year's newspaper corpus and more than 5,000 photographs — useful as a lead for locating period digital press material.

Assume Večer imagery is **copyrighted/reference-only unless an item explicitly states otherwise**.

### RTV Slovenija archival material
https://arhiv.rtvslo.si/osamosvojitev/

RTV's independence archive provides a dated 1991 chronology plus year sections through the rest of the decade. It includes television material and creator recollections; Maribor-specific reporting includes TV Maribor crews and Pohorje/Maribor events.

Use moving images to study:

- traffic flow and vehicle mix;
- crowd density and body language;
- street furniture from multiple angles;
- facade colours under contemporary broadcast cameras;
- public signage;
- period audio, speech and ambience;
- temporary event infrastructure.

Broadcast screenshots and footage remain reference material unless separate reuse rights are obtained.

#### TV Studio Maribor / Televizija Maribor — dedicated 1990s acquisition track

For the project's target decade, search under **TV Studio Maribor**, **Regionalni center RTV Maribor**, **RTV Slovenija — studio Maribor**, individual programme titles and creators — not only the modern channel name **TV Maribor**. The Maribor television operation had become a TV studio in the 1980s and produced regional and national material throughout the 1990s; the standalone regional channel/programme identity belongs to a later phase. This distinction materially improves archival discovery.

High-value material located in this research pass:

- **1991 Maribor tank-movement field footage** — Marjan Šrimpf's RTV recollection states that he and cameraman Jože Kramer filmed tanks leaving the Franc Rozman–Stane barracks, followed them along Cesta proletarskih brigad toward Pobrežje and returned the cassette to the Maribor studio, where the images were immediately transmitted: https://arhiv.rtvslo.si/osamosvojitev/www.rtvslo.si/osamosvojitev/novica/67.html . The same recollection identifies further TV Maribor filming of military barracks, Maribor airport, surrender scenes, collection centres and related locations. Locate the underlying archive tapes rather than treating the recollection page itself as the visual source.
- **Naš arhiv je živ** — https://365.rtvslo.si/oddaja/nas-arhiv-je-ziv/173250780 . This 2017 TV Maribor series explicitly revisits the station's much older archive. It should be screened episode by episode for 1990–1999 inserts, with every useful excerpt catalogued as a separate dated reference.
- **Mariborske zgodbe (1995)** — a 60-minute colour/B&W TV Studio Maribor city production documented by UKM: https://ukmdomblog.blogspot.com/2012/10/domofilm-lokalne-filmske-pripovedi-v.html?m=0 . This is a particularly strong acquisition target because the crew filmed through Maribor and intentionally included some of the city's contemporary state alongside historical reconstruction.
- **Na vrtu**, first broadcast **13 January 1996** — RTV's 30-year retrospective confirms the date and includes selected older inserts: https://365.rtvslo.si/raziskuj?c=show&l=SLO&q=na+vrtu&s=tv . Use early episodes for vegetation, gardens, domestic exteriors, clothing, tools and ordinary material culture; verify filming location per segment before calling an image Maribor-specific.
- **Leon Štukelj in njegovo stoletje (1998)** — 22-minute documentary produced by Regionalni center Maribor, TV program, with Maribor television camera crews; UKM documents the production and credits: https://ukmdomblog.blogspot.com/2012/10/domofilm-lokalne-filmske-pripovedi-v.html?m=0 .
- **Lent '99: Folkart** — a contemporary TV schedule explicitly lists this as an **oddaja TV studio Maribor**: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1999_62_L.pdf . This is a high-priority RTV-archive request because it may provide direct 1999 moving imagery of Lent crowds, temporary stages, signage, lighting and public space.
- **Poglej in zadeni (1993)** — WorldCat records a TV Studio Maribor VHS release: https://search.worldcat.org/cs/title/pop-godba-2-oddaja-poglej-in-zadeni/oclc/781154975 . Its value is primarily studio interiors, fashion, hairstyles, audiences, lighting, broadcast graphics and pop-culture props rather than city geometry.

### Systematic programme-listing sweep — 1990–1999
Newspaper television schedules are unusually effective for recovering the **names and transmission dates of programmes whose video is not indexed publicly online**. They should be treated as bibliographic evidence that a programme existed, not as substitutes for watching the programme itself.

The systematic sweep adds several important acquisition tracks:

- **Tele M** is explicitly listed as a Studio Maribor regional programme in **June 1990** and again as part of TV Slovenija's regional output in **March 1991**. These early current-affairs programmes are high-priority because routine local reporting is likely to contain ordinary streets, shops, roadworks, buses, institutions and neighbourhood environments. Sources: https://dolenjskilist.svet24.si/media/arhiv-pdf/dl/1990/DL_1990_06_07_23_2129.pdf and https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1991_23_L.pdf .
- **Regionalni studio Maribor** appears repeatedly in national television schedules from **1993 through 1995**, often around 18:00. Confirmed examples occur in March and December 1993, April and December 1994, and February, April and June 1995. Treat this as a collection-level search key in the RTV archive rather than as one programme: retrieve the surviving regional bulletins and split useful reports into item-level references. Starting schedule: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1993_17_L.pdf .
- **Na Lentu** is a confirmed TV Maribor programme family in **1998**. Listings include a generic *Na Lentu* edition as well as **Folkart '98**, **Paquito D'Rivera** and **Dr. John** programmes. This is one of the strongest late-1990s acquisition tracks in the entire art archive because it can preserve moving views of Lent, the Drava riverfront, temporary stages, barriers, banners, audience circulation, night lighting, clothing and festival-era street furniture. Sources: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1998_65_L.pdf , https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1998_63_L.pdf , https://www.dlib.si/stream/urn%3Anbn%3Asi%3Adoc-7jsssllb/8ed9142c-5226-4fef-97d4-9ed6294d78d7/pdf and https://www.dlib.si/details/URN:NBN:SI:doc-KJE3LJW1 .
- **Poglej in zadeni** should be acquired as a series, not merely through the already catalogued 1993 VHS. Petar Radović's retrospective account says production at TV Studio Maribor began in February 1992 and exceeded one hundred programmes over roughly two and a half years: https://svet24.si/revija/stop/estrada/traci/tuji/zakaj-je-radovic-rezal-hribarja--364656 . Its primary production value is period clothing, hairstyles, studio architecture, furniture, lighting rigs, audience behaviour, broadcast graphics and pop-culture material.
- **Ljudje in zemlja** became increasingly a Maribor production: an institutional history reports that by **1998** the Maribor studio produced three monthly editions, while later schedules explicitly identify editions as TV Maribor productions. This makes the series a strong source for the city's rural fringe and north-eastern Slovenian context: secondary roads, farms, machinery, work clothing, gardens, orchards, field boundaries and domestic exteriors. Source: https://www.academia.si/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/diploma-m-2008-samira-gorisek.pdf .
- Additional confirmed productions include **Zgodba o stoletju (1993)** and **Kerblerjevi Haložani nekoč in danes — Podobe časa II (1995)**, while the **Vurberk '94** archive states that Radio and Televizija Maribor recorded the entire 25 June 1994 festival. These are contextual rather than direct city references, but they can fill gaps in regional clothing, interiors, rural material culture, stages, PA equipment and audience behaviour.

### Additional programme families recovered in the third sweep

- **Tomo Česen predstavlja (1992)** — contemporary climbing press documents a ten-part series made by Tomo Česen and Studio Alp for **Studio Maribor / Tele M**. Individual episode notices survive for the third, fifth, seventh and tenth episodes, and a later notice confirms the complete ten-part run and repeat transmission. Start with https://gore-ljudje.net/novosti/alpinisticne-novice-16-1992/ and cross-check the later notices for episode order. This is contextual rather than city-specific evidence, but it is a useful 1992 corpus for clothing, equipment, outdoor cinematography and TV Maribor-era production practice.
- **Slovenci po svetu (1999)** — a contemporary 26 February 1999 Australian-Slovenian newspaper interview with Marjan Šrimpf states that TV Studio Maribor had launched the new programme in **January 1999**. The article also documents Šrimpf travelling with a TV cameraman and filming reports in Adelaide, Melbourne and Sydney: https://www.glasslovenije.com.au/hasaArhiv_2016/knjigeRevijeCasopisi/glasSlovenije/Glas%20Slovenije199__002.pdf . Later sources describe it as a long-running TV Maribor programme. It is most useful for late-1990s studio/reportage style, clothing, equipment and graphics rather than Maribor street reconstruction.
- **4x4 — oddaja o ljudeh in živalih (1999)** — TV schedules confirm a February 1999 broadcast, while a COBISS personal bibliography records participation in a 4 November 1999 report. The strand later continued as **O živalih in ljudeh**. This makes the 1999 run a useful acquisition target for ordinary homes, gardens, veterinary spaces, clothing and regional domestic material culture. Schedule starting point: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1999_12_L.pdf .
- **Bingo, Bang (2 January 1999)** — a Gorenjski glas schedule explicitly labels this as an **oddaja TV Maribor**: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1998_102_L.pdf . It is a lower-priority studio-entertainment lead, potentially useful for set design, audience clothing, props, lighting and television graphics.
- The same New Year 1999 schedule also confirms **Na vrtu** as a TV Maribor production and places it immediately after the Maribor-produced entertainment slot. It should therefore be used to cross-check programme naming and transmission dates when requesting masters from RTV.

#### Schedule-to-master workflow

For every schedule-derived discovery:

1. retain the newspaper title, issue date, broadcast date/time and exact programme wording in the catalogue;
2. search RTV 365, RTV's internal/archive catalogue, UKM and physical-video catalogues for the matching title and date;
3. where a recurring slot has no episode title, search the **broadcast date plus “Regionalni studio Maribor” / “Tele M”**;
4. once footage is found, replace or supplement the schedule-level row with an item-level master/tape record and precise runtime;
5. watch the footage before assigning street, district, landmark or asset claims;
6. log useful shot intervals or timecodes in downstream asset research notes;
7. preserve the schedule row when it remains useful provenance for an otherwise poorly indexed tape.

This workflow is deliberately biased toward **ordinary reporting**. A short regional-news item about road repairs, a new shop, a bus route, a factory closure or a neighbourhood event may be more valuable to Marburgum than a polished city documentary because it records uncurated everyday surfaces and objects.

A useful secondary finding aid is Dušan Tomažič's **Regionalni center RTV Maribor 1980–1998** (2000), cited in later media research. A 2008 study based on it records that the studio gained a report/OB vehicle in 1996, began **Na vrtu** that year and by 1998 was producing three monthly editions of **Ljudje in zemlja** after Ljubljana ceased co-producing that programme. See: https://www.academia.si/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/diploma-m-2008-samira-gorisek.pdf and https://zofijini.net/mediji_mbhumanistika/ .

Acquisition procedure for TV Studio Maribor material:

1. search RTV 365 and the older RTV independence archive by **programme title, creator, cameraman, venue and event**, not just by year;
2. search UKM local-film programming/catalogue traces for titles that survive on VHS or other physical carriers;
3. use contemporary newspaper TV schedules to establish that a programme existed and its transmission date, then request the underlying master from RTV;
4. for archival compilations, log the **original footage date**, not the retrospective broadcast date;
5. separate studio entertainment, staged reconstruction and documentary street footage in evidence notes;
6. assume all RTV footage is reference-only until explicit reuse permission is obtained.

### PTT / Telekom telephone directories — commercial world-state source

The **1991/92** and **1998** Slovenian telephone directories are unusually useful for reconstructing the commercial and institutional fabric behind the visual archive.

- The 1991/92 first volume includes **Maribor network group 062** and is explicitly based on subscriber data as of **15 January 1991**. Library of Congress holdings: https://guides.loc.gov/slovenia-telephone-directories/holdings .
- Contemporary 1998 Telekom advertising dates the Maribor-region directory release to **15 April 1998** and documents print, CD-ROM and online forms: https://arhiv.gorenjskiglas.si/digitar/16298754_1998_30_L.pdf .

Use these directories to answer questions such as:

- which shops, workshops, offices, clubs and institutions actually existed in a given year;
- period business names and categories for storefront/signage research;
- addresses that can be mapped to project buildings;
- telephone-number formats and PTT/Telekom graphic conventions;
- late-1990s office/computer props, including CD-ROM and early online-directory use.

A directory is **world-state evidence, not facade evidence**. Pair a business/address hit with a period photograph before reconstructing its storefront appearance.

https://www.dlib.si/

Use dLib for newspapers, magazines, photographs, posters, printed ephemera, audio/video and bibliographic discovery. Rights and online access vary per item. The Večer corpus is especially useful for text searches, advertisements, event dates and locating images that may need to be obtained from the rights holder or an archive.

High-value newly identified records include:

- **Maribor: mesto / university town / Universitätsstadt (1992)** — a ca. 15-minute VHS city film, https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3AMOV-OBTW3BF4 ; exceptionally valuable for moving traffic, street furniture, clothing, signage, architecture, colour and ambient city behaviour, although viewing is restricted to dedicated NUK terminals.
- **Luna nad Mississippijem (1993)** — SNG Maribor theatre poster, https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3AIMG-CJ75QNBC .
- **Jermanovo seme (1993)** — SNG Maribor audio cassette, https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ASND-U0AW71EM .
- **Lutkovne pesmice (1995)** — Lutkovno gledališče Maribor audio cassette, https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3ASND-XDNZAMIJ .
- **Junior world championship: Maribor, 23. 2.–1. 3. 1992** — 83 × 58 cm poster catalogued as 1991, https://www.dlib.si/details/URN%3ANBN%3ASI%3AIMG-IHES1W3Q ; useful for exact transition-period sports/event graphics and signage.

Do not ignore terminal-only records: for the archive, a stable URN/COBISS record can still be a high-value acquisition lead even when the media cannot be downloaded remotely.

### Pokrajinski muzej Maribor
https://museum-mb.si/zbirke/

The museum maintains photography and postcard collections. Much of its online showcase is older than the target decade, but the collection is valuable for identifying persistent architecture, objects, interiors and material culture, and for finding additional holdings not yet digitised.

### SIGIC — Slovenski glasbenoinformacijski center
https://www.sigic.si/
https://www.dlib.si/

SIGIC contributes photographic and music-related collections to dLib. A particularly useful discovery is **Jani Kenda's 1998 Festival Lent photography**, including a five-image set from the 2 July 1998 performance at Sodni stolp. These images carry CC BY-NC-ND metadata in dLib and provide unusually direct late-1990s reference for performers, clothing, temporary staging, lighting and the Lent cultural environment.

Search by photographer, performer, venue and year rather than only by "Maribor".

### Fotoklub Maribor / Fotogalerija Stolp
https://www.fotoklub-maribor.si/

Fotogalerija Stolp in the Židovski stolp is an important lead for 1990s Maribor photography. Exhibition records identify locally focused shows such as **Mariborski obrazi** (1997), **Mariborski fotografi** (1998), **Ex Tempore Lent '98** (shown in 1999), and work by established Maribor photographers.

The online exhibition trail does not always expose the underlying photographs. Treat exhibition records as acquisition leads for catalogues, leaflets, contact sheets, surviving prints and photographer estates.

### Raz_ume — Moderna galerija exhibition database
https://razume.mg-lj.si/

Raz_ume indexes Slovenian exhibitions, artists, venues, dates and, in some cases, surviving catalogues/leaflets. It is particularly useful for reconstructing the 1990s programme of **Fotogalerija Stolp**, Foto salon and other Maribor venues and for identifying photographers whose work may contain city, portrait, industrial or festival material.

Use it primarily as a provenance/discovery database; locate the actual photograph or catalogue before treating visual details as evidence.

### Zgodovinski arhiv Celje — archival guide
https://vodnik.zac.si/

Although based in Celje, the archive catalogue contains item-level Maribor material. A confirmed example is the **5 October 1997 colour photograph of the Maribor event marking 100 years of tennis** (SI_ZAC/0524/003_00473), which is publicly accessible and marked as not requiring permission for archival use.

Search by `Maribor`, exact years and event/institution names. "Permission not required" in an archival access record must not be interpreted automatically as public-domain copyright.

### Marprom / Certus historical transport archive
https://www.marprom.si/wp-content/uploads/90LetBus_Monografija_Web.pdf

### Europark / University of Maribor — 1998–1999 construction-state evidence
https://www.europark.si/si/o-nas/zgodovina/
https://dk.um.si/IzpisGradiva.php?id=3880&lang=slv

Europark is a critical **date-aware world-state case**, because present-day map data contains the completed shopping centre even though the project is set in the 1990s. Production rule for this site:

- pre-23 October 1998: do not back-project the completed modern mall footprint into the historical world;
- late 1998 through 1999: use a construction-state variant supported by dated evidence;
- the completed retail centre belongs to the post-target period, opening in August 2000;
- seek the 1999 thesis and period construction photography before authoring detailed temporary works, cranes, hoardings or site logistics.

## Acquisition priorities for modelling

When time is limited, collect in this order:

1. representative P3 Lent visual-slice viewpoints;
2. landmark facades and bridge/river interfaces;
3. streets with currently poor procedural geometry;
4. road surfaces, kerbs, pavements and street furniture;
5. period storefronts/signage and parked vehicles;
6. railway/industrial compounds;
7. residential districts and apartment-block grounds;
8. interiors and prop clusters;
9. vegetation/seasonal evidence;
10. night/weather reference.

A reference is most valuable when it answers a concrete production question, not merely because it is visually attractive.
