# /Game/Vegetation — the original game's tree and bush meshes

Copied from the original game, Magna Raedarum: Marburgum (`simon-gros/magna-raedarum-marburgum`, checkout
`30dd64c7`), `UnrealMarburgum/Content/Vegetation`, on 21 November 2026 for GFX-01 (VQ-022).

- **What they are.** Procedural meshes, one per species of the original's `project/data/vegetation.json`, built by its
  `tools/build_trees.gd` (Godot) at the species' natural size, exported as glTF
  (`UnrealMarburgum/SourceAssets/FromGodot/trees/*.glb`, listed with Git blob SHAs in that folder's `MANIFEST.json`,
  sha256 `3a897de88ae4f1d07463e6f2404da6e68177b8eb6e9b53f16581706b0df1c71c`) and imported into Unreal 5.8 by the original's Interchange pipeline, with a bark and
  a foliage material each.
- **Rights.** Marburgum-owned: generated by the original project's own code from its own species catalogue, no third-party
  geometry or texture.
- **Use.** `AMarburgumPlants` draws `SM_<species>` by path for every plant of
  `Content/Marburgum/Vegetation/lent_plants.json` (`lent-ground --mode plants`); `Config/DefaultGame.ini` always cooks
  this folder. Sixteen of the twenty meshes are used; `tree_broad`, `tree_poplar`, `tree_young` and `bush` are the
  original's general archetypes and no plant of the square uses them.
- **Changed here.** `scripts/create-weather-content.sh plants` (`MarburgumPlantContent`) re-saves each `SM_<species>` with
  its two slots pointed at our `Materials/M_PlantFoliage` and `M_PlantBark`, through `Species/<id>/MI_<id>_{Foliage,Bark}`
  in the original's colours: the glTF import's instances (`Species/<id>/<id>/Materials/*`, kept for provenance, no longer
  used) drew near-black in Lent's light. The geometry is unchanged.
- **Known limits.** The original calls these silhouettes placeholders: coarse foliage clumps, one LOD, no wind, no Nanite.
  Better silhouettes later from rights-cleared photographs through TRELLIS (ADR-071).
