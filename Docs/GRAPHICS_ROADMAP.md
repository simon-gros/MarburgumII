## Vision and boundaries

Deliver a visually convincing, historically grounded **1990s Maribor**—first as a polished, genuinely **playable Lent/Stari most/Drava vertical slice**, then as a reproducible graphical standard for the whole city. Quality must hold in **real multiplayer gameplay** with moving characters, weather, HUD and legible Slovene signs, not only in screenshots or Movie Render Queue output.

1. **Native quality first:** the world must look right with Unreal's renderer and cross-vendor TSR. NVIDIA-only rendering, frame generation and neural effects are optional client features. No vendor dependency in dedicated server, networking or gameplay state.
2. **Content before filters:** restore missing vegetation, furniture, landmarks, believable period façades, riverbank details, mission models and props before pursuing extreme post-processing.
3. **Profile before enabling:** Lumen rendering modes, Nanite, Substrate, MegaLights, ray tracing, PCG, third-party water plugins and VRAM-heavy effects are **candidates**, not assumed supported/enabled/suitable on Linux/Vulkan or in packages.
4. **Gameplay remains authoritative:** shaders cannot change collision, navigation, swimming/fishing, weather simulation, door interactions, crowd budgets, replication, account entry or persisted state. Preserve the existing HUD and accessibility gates.
5. **Rights and history:** first restore the original's Marburgum-owned vegetation/species and surveyed furniture. All new image-conditioned assets follow **Microsoft TRELLIS** and ADR-071 with source/rights/retopology/UV/PBR/collision/performance/owner review. Hunyuan3D is superseded. The future third-party city map is a separate, unapproved adoption decision.
6. **Acceptance only with evidence:** real packaged client(s), same source and cooked asset provenance, deterministic A/B views and personal owner testing. Offline 4K footage is never proof of real-time frame rate.

## Verified baseline and constraints (20 November 2026)

The current default **/Game/World/lent** is a **1,536 × 1,536 m, nine-window, always-loaded** development level, not a completed streamed city. The [ADR-072 visual audit](VISUAL_QUALITY_BACKLOG.md) surveyed 42 daylight views and inspected 1,571 static meshes. It identifies **870 mostly simple building shells, no trees/shrubs in the playable square, sparse street furniture, thin façade/bridge detail, uniform river water** and approximately **9.63 million always-loaded LOD0 triangles**, without applied Nanite/LOD/draw-distance optimization (VQ-028). The content deficiencies are more visible than hypothetical DLSS 5 enhancements.

In editor game mode on a free RTX 5080 at 1080p, most surveyed views took **2.55–3.22 ms GPU**, while three Pobrežje views took **7–8 ms**. These observations **do not qualify a packaged client**, mid-range GPU, latest code/asset revision, nighttime/rain, interiors, VRAM, crowds or multi-client gameplay. The separate recent clean packaged daytime crowd test also **failed at 11/28** on opposite banks: unrelated graphics work does not clear that P0 gameplay gate.

Current code already includes a dynamic sun/moon, skylight, sky atmosphere, height fog, clouds, weather-dependent material parameters and post-processing. First audit how the **playable level** configures them. The project's optional NVIDIA DLSS 4.5, DLAA, Reflex and Frame Generation lane requires Windows/D3D12 packaging validation; the tested Linux/Vulkan client falls back to TSR. The unofficial DLSS5ForUE5 source is present but the community-modified runtime is excluded and **must not be retrieved or distributed** (see [NVIDIA notes](RENDERING_NVIDIA.md)).

## Showcase reference route and work packages

The single most valuable reference is a short third-person walk beside the **Drava in Lent**, along the **Stari most approach** to a recognizably 1990s old-town frontage, with a separately tested ground-floor door/interior. Establish matching cameras at ground height, bridge deck, riverbank and elevated roof view; include NPC, vehicle and readable Slovene signage. Record any unavailable point rather than staging a fake feature.

- **Pass A — city completeness:** original trees/grass/flowers in their real environments; benches, bus shelters, bins, railings and building-specific props; mission actors and era-appropriate signage.
- **Pass B — convincing materials:** dry/wet cobblestones, asphalt, textured roofs, brick, plaster, decals, scaled window glass, visually plausible wear.
- **Pass C — lighting:** matched natural sunlight/shadows and soft overcast, dusk/night lamps, indoor exposure, fog/cloud and rain transition.
- **Pass D — motion and legibility:** moving crowd, vehicle, swim/fish, door, foliage wind; face/hair/surface response and no temporal ghosting or degraded text/HUD.
- **Pass E — cost and sign-off:** genuine multiplayer packaged walk-through with fresh source/asset hashes and before/after evidence, followed by **personal owner approval**.

## Benchmark protocol and provisional performance targets

Use a newly **packaged** UE 5.8.3 Client and dedicated Server from the **same clean SHA and sealed cooked asset set**, with zero concurrent GPU-heavy sessions. Capture engine/driver/RHI/GPU, resolution and render percentage, all active render settings (including any frame generation), world time/weather, scene/camera path and 1/2-client cases. Report **CPU/game/render/RHI/GPU p50/p95/p99**, RAM/VRAM, hitches, 1% lows where meaningful, frame pacing, visual artifacts, input latency when testing generated frames, and startup/restore behavior. Label editor-only evidence distinctly; a screenshot never proves a frame budget.

**Initial targets are aspirations, not existing measurements:** **1080p 60 FPS** on a declared mid-range card at Balanced, **1440p 60 FPS** on the high-end reference at High, with **1080p 30 FPS** graceful low-end fallback. For 60 FPS the *total* frame budget is **16.7 ms**; at 30 FPS it is **33.3 ms**. Test p95/p99 rather than hiding stutters behind an average. Neither DLSS Frame Generation nor Smooth Motion counts as a substitute for native-render/game-thread performance. **4K Cinematic is offline unless separate real-time benchmark evidence qualifies it.**

Matrix: noon/dusk/night/overcast/rain, dry/wet, static/moving camera, running NPC, driving, swimming/fishing, opening/closing a real door, exterior/interior transitions and UI/chat. Inspect water/reflections, hair, railings/wires, leaves, windows, signs (č/š/ž), ghosting, exposure jumps, shadow-cache movement, shader compilation stalls, missing mips and world gaps. Preserve same-build original and enhanced captures, not just curated success shots.
