# Research-backed additions and future gameplay roadmap — 20 November 2026

**Status:** public-research-informed **proposals**, not implementations, commitments or owner approvals. The current game remains Unreal Engine **5.8.3**, dedicated-server authoritative, fictionalized 1990s Maribor, with the original game's narratives and ecology preserved. New ideas must not reintroduce wanted stars/police chases or the excluded generated talent-tree system. **Priority:** first close the latest-head packaged/owner acceptance gates in the [evening review](DEVELOPMENT_REVIEW_2026-11-20_EVENING.md).

## Recommended new gameplay and engineering proposals

| Priority | Idea | First practical slice and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 R-01 — Named resident daily routine** | Let existing, server-owned NPCs occupy a home, a bus stop, a café and a daytime work post, consistent with the game's district/hour/attractor system. | Pick four to six named residents around Lent and one day/night cycle, preserving stable mission contacts; test two-client agreement, weather/day transitions and saved state. |
| **P1 R-02 — Furnished playable interior loop** | Make the current five cut/enterable ground-floor shells worth visiting before cutting more of the 581 eligible sealed shells. | Furnish Hotel Lent and vine museum with period-correct counters, doors, materials, lighting and one optional interaction each; test streaming, late join, 120 ms latency/1% loss, performance and personal approval. |
| **P1 R-03 — Photography album and historical comparison** | Build upon ADR-081's **already implemented** server-judged Photography, prints, rewards and Nika/Brane missions. | Add a personal photo journal, date/location/subject provenance, licensed “then and now” assignments and optional private sharing only with explicit consent; verify server ownership, identity and no leaking other players' photos. |
| **P1 R-04 — Analog 1990s communications** | Add historically plausible radios, cassette recorders and payphones, directly relevant to Chapter IV *Voices on Tape*. | One cassette/radio/payphone interaction with carefully licensed or newly recorded audio and a small mission clue; no real-phone dialing, copyrighted song recording or indiscriminate audio use. |
| **P1 R-05 — Small noncombat jobs** | Develop a meaningful everyday economy around existing Fishing, Trading, Photography, missions and the server-owned money/XP rules. | A courier delivery, small repair or market-stall job, one NPC and one inventory decision; test idempotent rewards, refunds/refusals and two-client stock/transaction contention. |
| **P1 R-06 — Historical accuracy pilot** | Use the existing 246 metadata references as a verified reference pipeline, focusing on period-appropriate Stari most/Lent and architectural details. | Audit an actually dated 1980–1990 reference plus an early-1990s local source, record date/rights/geolocation/uncertainty and approve one correctable signage/vegetation or frontage feature. No scraping protected photographs into public Git. |
| **P1 R-07 — Accessible bilingual UI** | English/Slovene and a working Settings panel already exist. | Optional scalable subtitles/notifications, stronger text contrast, controller/focus behavior and input remapping feasibility. Consider Epic Common UI **in one isolated UI surface**, not an unauthorized overhaul of the present UMG system. |
| **P1 R-08 — Reserved street interactions** | Existing bus-stop, shop and café attractions could support tiny queues, bus shelter waiting, benches and shared interactions. | One 2–3-slot reservation/timeout contract, deterministic on the server. Evaluate Unreal Smart Objects only as an adapter; retain current walk graph, NPC scheduling and authority. |
| **P2 R-09 — Calendar-driven local events** | Build civic life around existing time-of-day, weather, place labels and social gameplay. | A small, source-verified market morning or riverside event with time-limited decoration and dialogue, cap-safe pedestrian budgets and server-wide agreement. Avoid anachronistic institutions and unapproved live-service commitments. |
| **P2 R-10 — Streaming/HLOD pilot** | Nine windows are currently always loaded; further city growth needs measured content streaming, not a blind engine setting. | Copy one district to a separate World Partition/Data Layers/HLOD experiment; compare cold load, VRAM, GPU, actor streaming, door persistence, mission coordinates and network authority before any production-map switch. |
| **P2 R-11 — Network-interest profiling and security hardening** | Staged 2–3-client data and AES-256-GCM do not prove 250-player capacity or complete transport protection. | Unreal Networking Insights on 1/3/8 clients, p95 CPU/network/actor relevance, reconnect/fault injection and a threat model for keys/replay/Hello-token denial; then isolate an **Iris versus Replication Graph** evaluation without deploying both simultaneously. |
| **P2 R-12 — Weather, water and physical lighting** | Improve the Drava and night city now that a read-only GFX-00 baseline and ADR-082 hardware-Lumen decision exist. | A fixed-route A/B at noon/night/rain, current packaged baseline and separately cooked Vulkan SM6 hardware-RT variant, with unsupported-GPU fallback, p95/p99 GPU+CPU/VRAM and personal visual approval. |

## Online sources and design implications

**Engine documentation, Unreal Engine 5.8:** [World Partition](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine) covers distance-based level management; [Smart Objects](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/smart-objects-in-unreal-engine---overview) supports world-interaction reservations. These systems are reference ideas, **not current Marburgum II architecture**. [Common UI](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/common-ui-plugin-for-advanced-user-interfaces-in-unreal-engine) provides input routing, while [UMG optimization](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/optimization-guidelines-for-umg-in-unreal-engine) favors profiling layouts rather than indiscriminate widget proliferation. [Networked-game testing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/testing-and-debugging-networked-games-in-unreal-engine) explains diagnostics; [Migrate to Iris](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/migrate-to-iris-in-unreal-engine) notes its distinction from Replication Graph. No performance gain is assumed without a matched benchmark.

**Accessibility:** [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/) emphasizes customizable controls, readable text, adequate contrast and captioning. Only introduce features relevant to this game's actual voice/text, with real players testing the results.

**Local historical sources:** Kamra/University Library of Maribor's [Stari most 1913–2013 collection](https://www.kamra.si/digitalne-zbirke/mariborski-stari-most-med-1913-2013/) identifies a **1980–1990** reference grouping; [Maribor historical postcards](https://www.kamra.si/digitalne-zbirke/razglednice-maribora-iz-19-in-20-stoletja/) offer local visual context. The image date, place and reuse rights must be verified; a public reference is **not** automatic permission to copy the image, train, generate a derivative, or publish it.

## Owner decision and staged acceptance protocol

Each **P1 candidate** needs a short owner-reviewed specification covering legacy parity, gameplay value, 1990s provenance, server authority, data migration, localization, asset rights, package/freshness test and exact acceptance criteria. Each **P2 engine feature** belongs to a reversible branch or copied map and a measured head-to-head baseline. Record statuses separately: **proposed**, **source implemented**, **editor qualified**, **packaged qualified**, **personally owner approved**. The chosen image-to-3D workflow remains **Microsoft TRELLIS**, not Hunyuan3D. This research document itself implements **none** of its proposals.
